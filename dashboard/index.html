What it does not claim

It does not say a token will be issued, or when. It prices the record as it stands: a protocol that records no token is a candidate, never a promise. The watchlist is the house's floor; the board carries it whole and adds every smaller candidate the snapshot holds.

The second lens

Farm window is 0–100 and never moves a rank: 45% seven-day TVL momentum, 35% youth, 20% size. Rank says what the launch is worth; window says whether to start this month.
